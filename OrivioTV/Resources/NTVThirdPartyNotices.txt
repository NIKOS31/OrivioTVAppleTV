NTVNuvioRuntime.swift is a Swift adaptation of formatPlaybackTime and
formatPlaybackRuntime in PlayerLayout.kt. Seconds replace milliseconds;
non-finite values and malformed engine durations are guarded explicitly.
The elapsed/remaining-time interaction is adapted from PlayerControlActions.kt.
This is a partial port. Nuvio's executable and playback engine are not bundled.
